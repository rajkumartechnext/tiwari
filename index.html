Public service starts with showing up. Jitendra Tiwari has held public office since 2015, first as Mayor of Asansol and then as the MLA for Pandaveswar, a coal-belt constituency in West Bengal where jobs, safety and industry are everyday concerns.

Biography

Jitendra Tiwari: MLA for
Pandaveswar

Jitendra Kumar Tiwari, also spelled Tewari in official records, represents Pandaveswar in the West Bengal Legislative Assembly as a member of the Bharatiya Janata Party. His public life took shape in the Asansol-Durgapur belt. On 13 October 2015 he became Mayor of Asansol, a post he held until December 2020. A year into that term he contested the Pandaveswar seat for the first time. In 2016, he won with 68,600 votes, a margin of 5,470 over the CPI(M) candidate.

Constituency

Pandaveswar, in Paschim Bardhaman’s coal belt, thrives on mining, local business and emerging industries.

Public service

Mayor of Asansol (2015 to 2020). MLA, Pandaveswar (2016 to 2021 and from 2026).

Public Service & Achievements

A Better Future for Pandaveswar

People here want what people everywhere want: a job close to home, a road that holds up in the monsoon, a clinic that has a doctor in it. That is the standard Jitendra Tiwari measures his work against.

01.Employment & Youth Opportunities

In October 2026 the MLA spoke publicly about an investment proposal of around Rs 1,000 crore for Pandaveswar, which he said would create a large number of jobs. After the 2026 result he described the mandate as a call to reindustrialise Bengal and create employment. His focus is simple: industry that hires local young people.

02.Better Roads & Public Facilities

On the night of 26 October 2026, twelve workers were trapped about 200 feet underground in a mine shaft at Kumardihi, Pandaveswar. Jitendra Tiwari was at the site through the night while police, disaster management teams, a mining expert and mine authorities worked together.

03. Faith and Community:

While serving as MLA, he took the initiative to build a Ram temple in Pandaveswar, and during the 2021 campaign he promised senior citizens of the constituency a free trip to Ayodhya if elected.

Mission for West Bengal
Employment

Opportunities for Youth

Bring industry and skilling programmes to Paschim Bardhaman so young people can find work without leaving their families behind.
Education

Access to Quality Education

Push for better schools and learning opportunities across the constituency, from the town to the village.
Healthcare

Accessible Public Healthcare:

Keep essential health services within reach, with attention to mining-area communities who live far from city hospitals.
Development

Better Civic Infrastructure

Focus on roads, drainage and public facilities, and make full use of central and state schemes, something he has argued for since his days as Mayor of Asansol.
Vision for the People
Public welfare

Support Every Family

Every household in Pandaveswar should be able to reach the public services it is entitled to, without a middleman and without a long wait.
Local economy

Strengthen Local Livelihoods

A mining belt does not have to depend on mining alone. Support for small business, new industry and local hiring keeps income and talent in the constituency.
Accountability

Listen to Every Community

Pandaveswar has voted both ways in recent elections, and the margins have been narrow. That is a reminder to keep listening to everyone, including people who voted for someone else.
Shared future

Inclusive State Development

A stronger Bengal has to include the coal belt. He wants Pandaveswar to be part of the state's industrial revival from day one.
