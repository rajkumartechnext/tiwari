Biography

Introduction

Jitendra Tiwari is the Member of the West Bengal Legislative Assembly from Pandaveswar and a member of the Bharatiya Janata Party. Born in Asansol, he has spent two decades in public life, guided by a simple belief: leadership begins with listening.

His journey began at the grassroots in 2006, working alongside people to understand their concerns and aspirations. In October 2015, he was elected Mayor of Asansol Municipal Corporation, a role in which he helped steer the city’s civic administration and development until December 2020. In 2016, he was elected to the West Bengal Legislative Assembly from Pandaveswar.

He returned to the Assembly in the 2026 election with the people’s mandate in Pandaveswar. Today, he works for better public facilities, quality education, accessible healthcare and equal opportunities for every citizen of the constituency.

Early Political Journey

Jitendra Tiwari’s journey began on the ground, not in an office. Starting in 2006, he spent his early years among the people, listening to their worries and hopes. Those conversations shaped the kind of leader he wanted to become.

Growing Roots in the Community

By 2011, his work had taken deeper root across the region. Year after year, the trust he earned in neighbourhoods and communities became the foundation of his public life.

Mayor of Asansol

In October 2015, he was elected Mayor of Asansol Municipal Corporation. He took on the everyday work of running a city and served in the role until December 2020.

First MLA Election Victory

In 2016, the voters of Pandabeswar elected him to the West Bengal Legislative Assembly. It was his first win as an MLA, and it gave him a bigger platform to carry the people’s concerns to the state level.

A New Political Chapter

On 2 March 2021, he joined the Bharatiya Janata Party and began a new chapter. He contested the Pandabeswar seat that year in a closely fought election, and he came away more focused on public service and development.

Return to the Legislative Assembly

In 2026, the people of Pandabeswar sent him back to the Assembly. He returned to represent them again, and he is committed to the region’s development.

Personal Philosophy

My Mission

My mission is to serve the people of Pandabeswar with honesty and humility. I listen to their concerns and turn them into real action.
I work to improve public facilities, education and healthcare so that every citizen can live with dignity. Whatever the need, my door stays open.

My Vision

I see a Pandabeswar where progress reaches every home and no one is left behind. It is a community with good schools, reliable healthcare and fair opportunities for all. I want to build growth that lasts, so the next generation inherits a stronger, more hopeful future.
